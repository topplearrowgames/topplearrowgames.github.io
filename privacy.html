Topple Arrow – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Topple Arrow (the "Game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The Game is published by Alan Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to topplearrowgames@gmail.com.

Short version: we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. There are no player accounts, chat or social features. The Game saves your progress and shows optional rewarded ads, and our service partners process some device data to make that work.

1. Information the Game processes

WhatWhyWho processes it
Game progress (level, coins, bows owned, settings) and a random player ID created when the Game first starts To save your progress and keep a backup copy of it Stored on your device and backed up with Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save)
Device and usage data: device model, operating system, language, IP address, ad interactions, and the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking To show rewarded ads, measure them, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the ad networks it uses, such as Unity Ads

Your privacy choices (personalised ads on/off, tracking permission) To respect them Stored on your device and passed to our ad partners

2. Advertising

The Game is free and is supported by rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example for extra arrows or bonus coins. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay.

  • Tracking permission (iOS): the Game asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ad partners do not receive your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Personalised ads: you can turn personalised ads off at any time in the Game under Settings > Personal Ads. When it is off, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests. Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked before any personalised ads are shown.

Our partners' privacy policies: Unity (including LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads), Apple.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Performance of our agreement with you: saving progress and delivering purchases.
  • Consent: personalised ads and tracking. You can withdraw consent at any time in the Game's Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Legitimate interests: non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and keeping the Game working.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records where the law requires it.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers named above, and only so they can provide their services. We also share data where the law requires it, or if the Game is transferred to a new owner, who would then be bound by this policy. Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep data

Game progress is kept while you play. We keep the backup only as long as it is needed to run the Game, and we delete it if you ask. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and consumer law requires.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Under Turkish law (KVKK) and the GDPR you may also complain to your data protection authority. California residents may ask what personal information we hold and ask us to delete it, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

To make a request, email topplearrowgames@gmail.com and tell us your device model and the approximate date you started playing. We will reply within 30 days. Deleting the Game removes the data stored on your device. It does not delete the backup on Unity's servers, so ask us if you want that removed.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with reputable providers. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top, and for important changes we will let you know in the Game.
